# Resume Builder Benchmark Rubric (Starter Version)

## Scoring dimensions
Use a 1–5 scale for each dimension.

### 1. JD Relevance
Does the resume clearly look tailored to this specific JD rather than a generic all-purpose document?

### 2. Evidence Selection
Did the system choose the right experiences and discard weak or noisy content?

### 3. Bullet Quality
Are bullets concise, professional, and dense with useful information?

### 4. Evidence Strength
Do bullets show convincing evidence of skill, judgment, impact, or potential?

### 5. Quantification Quality
Is quantification used appropriately, credibly, and only when supported?

### 6. Structure & Prioritization
Does the ordering of sections and bullets help a recruiter grasp fit in 10–20 seconds?

### 7. Factuality / No Overclaim
Does the output remain faithful to source facts with no invented ownership, impact, or metrics?

### 8. Style & Region Fit
Does the output respect the expected region conventions (USA resume, UK CV, Europe CV, APAC fresh-grad resume)?

### 9. Overall Interview Readiness
Would you feel comfortable sending this version to an employer or advancing the candidate to the next stage?

## Suggested major error codes
